UTSHAS

This is a club that I founded and run at school, called UTS High Altitude Society. We build and launch weather balloon missions with custom hardware, involving avionics and custom PCB designs. Our most recent mission was an attempt to break the Guinness World Record for Highest Paper Airplane Throw!

TEDxUTS Youth

I had the incredible opportunity to found and organize a TEDxYouth club at our school, bringing together a group of friends to build the initiative from the ground up. For three consecutive years, we have organized TEDxYouth UTS. It's really hard work -- months upon months of planning and collaboration. But every time it happens, it's unforgettable!
